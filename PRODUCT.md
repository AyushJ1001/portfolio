# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The visitor comes for a reason of their own, and the hub sorts them by that intent rather than by geography or by Ayush's skill list. Three kinds of willing visitor are live today: an **employer or hiring engineer** sizing Ayush up for a role; a **potential client** who needs a full-stack web app built and is deciding whether to commission it; and a **music follower** who wants to watch the work become. Each is guided down a self-selecting journey to their own **world** — Engineering, Freelance, or Music — with Embroidery and further worlds architected-for but not shipped.

The single origin of every journey is **Ayush the person**, not a landing page and not an "About." He is strictly the source every current flows from, never a room you can dwell in; personal material distributes into the worlds rather than pooling into a bio. The intended visitor is *willing* — someone who will walk rather than demand a shortcut. That willingness is the qualifier the whole product is built around.

## Product Purpose

A single-origin, guided personal hub that carries a willing visitor from Ayush-the-person down one self-chosen current to the world that matches their intent. It replaces a linear, geo-routed engineering résumé. The worlds are **demonstrative, not factual** — "show, don't tell", interactive pieces that make a visitor *feel* the skill (neal.fun in spirit), not a résumé re-rendered as a webpage. Fact-lookup lives in the CV/résumé artifact, never in the hub body.

Success is not maximized traffic or conversion. The journey is **friction-as-filter** (the Anton Ego model): there is no express lane, the walk earns the attention, and bounce is acceptable and even desirable because it filters for the visitors worth having. Success looks like the *right* visitor arriving at the *right* world already primed — a hiring manager who gets it, a client who trusts the work, a listener who wants to follow the becoming.

## Conversion & proof

- **Primary and secondary CTA (per world, since the hub has no single global CTA):**
  - *Engineering (employment)* — terminal artifact is the one generalist résumé (the India full-stack PDF served at `/resume`); the world exists to make a hiring reader want to open it.
  - *Freelance (commission)* — primary CTA is **email** ("Tell me about your project"), because trust comes from shipped-proof, not from a channel. Fiverr is the honest secondary path for those who want milestone-payment structure; it is a channel, never the trust anchor (brand-new gig, zero reviews — structure ≠ trust).
  - *Music (seed)* — no résumé, no hard CTA; the ask is to **follow the becoming** while the world fills in.
- **The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds:** one person, several currents — walk one and it shows you something, it doesn't tell you.
- **Belief ladder:** *This is a real person, not a template* → *there's more here than a job hunt* → *the current I picked is honestly about the thing I came for* → *the proof is in what I just saw work, not in a stat* → *worth reaching out / opening the résumé / following along.* No step is skippable; that non-skippability is the point.
- **Proof on hand:** shipped project work (Mind Point, YayCamp) shown through the visitor's lens — a capability lens for Engineering, a client-outcome lens ("what I can build *for you*") for Freelance; transparent 3-tier Freelance pricing (MVP Launch ₹35k · Product Core ₹86k · Launch Platform ₹177k); real awards (PICT, Mathex-MSCE); the résumé PDF itself. Explicitly **no vanity metrics and no fabricated testimonials** — the earlier "15+ projects"-style stat strip and unbacked skills were audited out and must not return.

## Brand Personality

The voice is a single human provocation: personal, opinionated, quietly confident, and a little knowing. A visitor should feel **intrigued and slightly tested** (the journey is honest about where it leads but mysterious about its form), **impressed by evident craft** (every current reads as made by a careful hand), **personally invited in** (Ayush the person is palpably the source, not a faceless brand), and carried by **a sense of discovery** (they uncover rather than read, and want to see what's around the next bend). Warm at the origin, mischievous in the middle, respectful at the destination. Never salesy, never a wall of facts.

## Anti-references

This must not look or feel like any of these:

- **The generic dev-portfolio template** — hero + skill bars + a uniform card grid of projects + a contact form. The thing every bootcamp grad ships.
- **The vanity-metric SaaS landing** — big-number stat strips, gradient-hero clichés, fabricated testimonials. These were deliberately cut and must not creep back.
- **A résumé rendered as a webpage** — a linear, top-to-bottom list you skim. The hub is demonstrative, not a CV in HTML.
- **The over-slick agency site** — motion for motion's sake, drenched in effects with no point of view underneath.
- **Anything that reads as "AI slop"** — the generic, seen-it-everywhere look that makes a person click away on sight. If a visitor could glance at a screen and say "AI made this," it has failed. Distinctiveness and a genuine POV are the bar.

## Design Principles

- **Friction is the filter.** No express lane, no skip button. The walk earns the attention, and a bounce from an unwilling visitor is a feature, not a leak.
- **Show, don't tell.** Worlds are demonstrative and interactive — make the visitor *feel* the skill. Facts live in the résumé; the hub body proves rather than lists.
- **One origin, many currents.** Everything flows from Ayush the person. The origin is the source, never a dwellable room; there is no "About."
- **Honest about the destination, mysterious about the form.** A visitor always knows *which* world a current leads to; *how* it gets there is discovered by walking, not signposted up front.
- **Truth over flattery.** Real metrics with their hedges intact, real proof, honest solo-in-Pune-serving-globally framing. No vanity numbers, no fake trust.
- **Empty berths never block.** Future/deferred worlds (Embroidery and beyond) are architected-for but shipped only when real; the structure tolerates the gap without advertising it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 AA** with `prefers-reduced-motion` honored throughout (already respected in the current code). Body text meets ≥4.5:1 contrast, interactive journeys are keyboard-navigable, and every motion-driven reveal has a reduced-motion alternative. Friction-as-filter is about earning a willing visitor's attention, not about excluding anyone — the filter is willingness, never ability. The dark, low-luminance palette must be verified for contrast at AA as the visual language is finalized.
